## Description

Every questionnaire can upload its sampling frame. Clarified by the owner
(2026-08-15, finding F-015): "sample" = the **sampling frame** of a survey —
the known list of target respondents (person or organization) for a targeted
survey. Uploaded via an Excel file, with a fixed column format. This
subsequently powers invitation distribution: each row becomes a sample entry
that can receive a unique link (TKT-001 flow).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Questionnaire can store a sampling frame (rows of organization_name +
      contact) — persisted in the DB (replaces/augments the free-text
      sampleEmails list)
- [ ] Upload accepts an Excel (.xlsx) file with columns: `organization_name`,
      `contact` (contact may be a phone number OR an email)
- [ ] Upload validates the format: required columns present, rows non-empty,
      contact values plausible (email regex or phone); clear per-row errors
- [ ] Uploaded entries are visible/editable in the questionnaire settings
- [ ] Sampling-frame entries feed the unique-link distribution flow
      (generate invitations per entry contact where contact is an email;
      non-email contacts flagged for other distribution)
- [ ] Unit + integration tests green

## Notes

Clarification resolved 2026-08-15 (F-015): the sample is the sampling frame
for targeted surveys where the respondent is known (person or organization).
